## Corrected Brand Direction — Echoo Blue / White

**Design Movement:** A spacious blue-and-white product bulletin inspired by the supplied paired-ellipse logo. It combines calm technology, rounded signal forms, and precise release-note structure.

**Core Principles:** Use asymmetry to make the release feel active rather than templated. Let the product story lead with purposeful typography. Use clear borders, ruled divisions, and compact metadata to establish trust. Keep each download path instantly legible.

**Color Philosophy:** White is the dominant space, deep Echoo blue establishes clarity and trust, and a lighter blue carries secondary signal moments. Orange and the earlier ink-and-bone direction are expressly removed.

**Layout Paradigm:** A split editorial masthead leads into horizontal release bands. A narrow label rail provides orientation while large content columns carry the narrative, platform choices, and feature notes. This intentionally avoids a conventional centered-card landing-page composition.

**Signature Elements:** The supplied two-ellipse mark; curved blue signal forms that echo its geometry; and light blue release bands against calm white space.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions should feel like operating precise audio equipment. Links underline decisively; download buttons compress slightly when pressed; section navigation scrolls directly without visual theatrics.

**Animation:** Use short 160–240ms opacity and transform transitions for hover and entry states. On load, the metadata and waveform ease in with a small vertical lift. Disable all nonessential motion for reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** DM Sans establishes a clean, contemporary product voice; Source Serif 4 supplies an editorial counterpoint for feature explanations. Labels use compact uppercase DM Sans with generous tracking. Headlines are set large, tight, and confidently left-aligned.

**Brand Essence:** Echoo Studio is the cross-platform live-audio studio for creators who need broadcast-grade control without a broadcast-grade barrier. **Direct, audible, dependable.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are concise and declarative; CTAs name the exact action; microcopy removes uncertainty. Example lines: “Your live room, in sharper focus.” and “Download the build that fits your desk.” Generic filler such as “Welcome” and “Get started” is prohibited.

**Wordmark & Logo:** A heavy custom E built from two offset audio pulses, sitting beside an optically spaced ECHOO wordmark. The icon is a standalone symbol rather than the brand name set in a default font.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Echoo Blue — #164F9D**, with **Soft Echoo Blue — #9BB7D9** and white surfaces.

## Style Decisions

The release site uses the corrected **Echoo Blue / White** direction throughout. It will use the user-supplied paired-ellipse logo as the only brand mark. The release version, platform labels, and download status are treated as first-class editorial information.

## Main Website Extension

The main homepage will use the same white, Echoo Blue, and Soft Echoo Blue system, but will shift its focus from a version announcement to the broader product story: why Echoo exists, how a live room moves, and how the desktop app fits the creator workflow. The v1.0.3 release remains a dedicated `/release` page, reachable from the global navigation, the desktop section, and the homepage release callout.
